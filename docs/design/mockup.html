Duffel: direction A, lighter icons

Icons now appear only where they carry meaning: the trip details line, group headers, carry-on and no-suitcase badges, form fields and trip types. Item rows have no category icons, and collapsed groups show only their count. The left phone is light and the right is dark; both are clickable and stay in sync. The names are made-up sample data.

  • Item rows: checkbox, name, then a bag or person line underneath. No per-item icons.
  • Collapsed groups keep only the count on the right.
  • Bag options are explicit: a real bag, No bag (car seat, stroller: travels outside any bag) or Decide later. Grouped by bag, "No bag" and "No bag yet" are separate sections. "No bag" items never count as luggage or as unassigned.
  • Filters: two side-by-side switches, All / Packed / To pack and All / Carry-on / Luggage, which combine. A quiet "Group by Person · Bag" line sits below, with "Clear filters" when either switch isn't on All. Luggage leaves out carry-on and no-suitcase items.
  • Carry-on belongs to the bag, not the item. Each bag is marked carry-on or not, and an item counts as carry-on when it's in a carry-on bag. The edit sheet has no carry-on switch; picking a bag sets it. When grouped by bag, carry-on bags show a small "Carry-on" tag. When grouped by person, items in a carry-on bag show a small teal backpack badge. Things that never go in a suitcase (car seat, stroller) get a dashed badge.
  • Details line: calendar, nights, travelers and place icons. Trips list: a plane or car tile by trip type. The trip tabs and filters are text only.
  • Form fields in the edit and new-trip sheets each lead with an icon, so the sheet scans quickly.
